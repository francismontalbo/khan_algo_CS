# Learning source audit

**Check date: 15 October 2026.** This is a learning-resource addition, not a new novelty review or a new algorithm benchmark.

## Validation boundary

University and project-maintainer pages were preferred. Video presence and topic were checked against their publisher pages, not random mirrors, keyword-only search hits, or event announcements without recordings. No claim of transcript-level or end-to-end video audit is made. Third-party playback may fail even when the publisher link is legitimate.

Written guidance was checked for its stated scope; no blanket certification of every linked page is implied. Older software and event recordings carry historical/version cautions. The NASA FITS primer was replaced in the learning collection because its datatype discussion is dated. The scoped Astropy opening and header introduction is used instead.

Primary research sources and local teaching examples remain separate from tutorials. No direct tutorial was inferred for AHRSZ, Pearce–Kelly variants, the exact XLA list scheduler revisions, or the local counter reductions. They receive source links and clearly marked prerequisites.
